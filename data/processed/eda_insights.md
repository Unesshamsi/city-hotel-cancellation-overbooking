# EDA — 8 charts and managerial insights

**Chart 1.** Bookings made 365+ days ahead cancel 51% of the time versus 11% within a week; lead time is therefore an early operational risk signal.

**Chart 2.** Non-Refund bookings cancel 97% historically, but this is concentrated in structured/allotment channels; it should not be treated as causal evidence that a consumer deposit fails.

**Chart 3.** Segment differences are operationally useful but can proxy channel, contract and allotment structure; treatment decisions should not assume a segment effect is purely guest behaviour.

**Chart 4.** The 2017 Jan–Aug period has a higher cancellation rate (34%) than 2016 (29%), while 2015 is only Jul–Dec (22%); the partial-year coverage means this is a descriptive historical signal, not proof of a monotonic trend.

**Chart 5.** Median ADR is €111 for cancelled versus €102 for completed bookings; higher-value reservations deserve attention when room value at risk is considered.

**Chart 6.** Non-Refund bookings have a median lead time of 198 days versus 49 for No Deposit, consistent with structured advance allotments rather than a clean consumer-policy comparison.

**Chart 7.** 98% of Non-Refund bookings are from PRT and 91% are Groups/Offline TA-TO; this supports treating the category as a process/channel signal rather than a consumer experiment.

**Chart 8.** Bookings arriving within 3 days of a holiday cancel 33% versus 28% for 30+ days away; this is useful as planning context, not a causal holiday effect.
